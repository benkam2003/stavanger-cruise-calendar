As of 23 November 2026, unchanged data from the previous app. No live synchronisation. Nationality flags indicate estimated target markets, not confirmed passenger majorities or ship registration. Itineraries for three Februaryy calls are based on published schedules; other calls offer a monthly itinerary link and manual entry. Market estimate source: Carnival Investor Day 2023. Capacities are estimates, not actual passenger counts. Calls with missing times are exported as all-day events. Times are local to Stavanger (Europe/Oslo).
